# Literary Agent Profiles — *String Theory* Submission Strategy

*Detailed personality, taste, and submission-approach profiles for 40 literary agents identified as strong fits for an upmarket literary novel with speculative elements. Compiled in two batches. Full JSON source data retained in raw/ for reference.* (source: Top 20 Agent Profiles.json, Top 21-40 agents for string theory.json — last_updated: 2026-03-12)

The profiles are organized by submission tier (1 = highest priority) within each batch. The fundamental submission frame for *String Theory*: upmarket literary fiction with speculative elements, formal musical architecture (four-movement chapters, tempo markings), physics/music intersection, father-son grief, buried institutional suppression, and a cinematic high-concept premise (a physicist's performance becomes the only surviving video after a solar storm, watched by billions).

## Batch 1 — Top 20 Agents

### Tier 1

**Ernie Chiara** | Fuse Literary
Archetype: The Writer-Agent. Writes speculative fiction himself; reads like a practitioner. Responds to voice above all — distinctive narrative presence on page 1. Loves "the inexplicable rendered in precise literary prose." Will understand *String Theory*'s musical architecture (tempo markings, four-movement Chapter 1 structure, Sarabande climax) at a craft level. Loses interest when speculative elements feel grafted rather than organic. *Approach:* Lead with voice and the 'inexplicable' element — the frequency, the visions. Emphasize that the speculative grows organically from emotional truth.

**Sarah Brooks** | Darley Anderson & Associates
Archetype: The Musician-Agent. Music degree (Nottingham) and piano performance diploma; will understand tempo markings, four-movement structure, and Sarabande on a technical level that few agents can match. Reads with an inner ear — prose must have rhythm and cadence. Responds to musicality in language and structure. *Approach:* Reference the musical structure explicitly. She'll recognize immediately that this book was written by someone who understands music from the inside.

**Samantha Fabien** | Root Literary

## Strategic Patterns Across All Profiles

Three consistent patterns emerge from the full agent set:

**The musical architecture is a tier-1 differentiator.** Sarah Brooks (music degree), PJ Mark (form-benders), and Henry Dunow (Landenwich precedent) will respond to the tempo markings and four-movement structure at a practitioner level. Lead with this in queries to musical or formally ambitious agents; ground it in emotional truth for agents who prioritize character over form.

**The lived-idea framing beats the explainer.** Across agents like Chris Parris-Lamb, Kent Wolf, and Dylan Haston, the recurring requirement is that the frequency physically invades David's body — it is not a scientific concept he studies. The query must embody the idea, not describe it.

**Balance in the query: intimate ↔ mythic.** Julie Barer, Angeline Rodriguez, and Aram Fox all require that the father-son grief and the David-Celeste relationship be present as the emotional core even when the cosmic stakes are on the table. The personal must ground the mythic, not compete with it.
