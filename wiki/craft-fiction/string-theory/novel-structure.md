# String Theory — Novel Structure

*String Theory* is a novel by Gabriel McPherson. Draft 6.7 (built 2026-07-11) stands at **107,022 words across an Overture and 27 chapters**. Chapters carry musical form designations — subtitles and scene headings that map classical compositional architecture onto narrative structure. The 27-chapter build resulted from splitting the original two long opening chapters (old Draft 6.6 Chs 1–2, ~22,000 words) into eight focused shorter chapters and further splitting old Ch 4 into two.

*Source: `manifest.json` at `https://gabrielmcp78.github.io/string-theory-chapters/manifest.json`, retrieved 2026-07-16. Scene-level data from the manifest is authoritative. For exact prose retrieval see the [Manuscript Manifest](manuscript-manifest.md) routing hierarchy.*

> **Draft 6.6 → 6.7 structural change.** Draft 6.6 had 20 chapters. Draft 6.7 has 27. The first two long chapters of 6.6 were each split into four focused chapters; old Ch 4 was split into two. Old Chs 3 and 5–20 are now Chs 9 and 12–27 respectively, shifted by seven positions. The [Chapter Mapping](#chapter-mapping-66--67) section at the bottom documents the full correspondence.

## Structural Logic

**Ch 5 (Now… Then)** uses a SUBJECT/ANSWER alternation between present-day Buffalo and MIT April/May 2005 — a fugal device applied to the chapter's intercutting timeline structure.

**Ch 11's "The bench" (scene 11.3)** is the buried capsule excavation scene. The Walk sequence (A through E across Chs 10–11) structures the approach and aftermath as a suite with interstitials.

**Ch 13 (Visions and Burdens… of Knowing)** opens each scene with a historical figure: Pythagoras, Émilie du Châtelet, Helmholtz/Chladni, Isaac Newton. The chapter uses the resonance tradition as the frame through which David's institutional collapse (The Purge, scene 3) is contextualized. See [Chapter 13 — Visions and Burdens](chapter-13-visions-and-burdens.md) for the full scene-by-scene breakdown.

**Ch 17 (Fractional Currency)** is the first chapter date-stamped November 28, 2026 — David's 47th birthday, the convergence date. Everything from Ch 17 onward is the novel's present-tense climax.

**Ch 20 scene 20.5 ("THE WEIGHT OF NAMES")** is date-stamped September 1971 — the 1971 Project ARGO flashback, Eleanor Guare's origin story.

**Ch 24 (Endless Possibilities / Collapsed Reality)** uses triple repetition of the scene heading "Observed superposition" — a formal quantum physics pun on David's state.
