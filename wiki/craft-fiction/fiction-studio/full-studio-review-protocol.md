# Fiction Studio 2.0 — Full Studio Review Protocol

*Canonical protocol established 2026-07-18 by ChatGPT/Atlas at Gabriel McPherson’s direction.*

## Purpose

A Full Studio Review is the highest-order Fiction Studio review for a chapter, sequence, part, manuscript, or high-stakes artistic decision. It coordinates specialized departments without fragmenting authorship, converting diagnosis into covert revision, or allowing market logic, local elegance, or procedural neatness to erase the work’s identity.

The protocol exists to improve judgment, not to generate more commentary. It should be invoked only when multiple jurisdictions are genuinely needed.

The governing sentence is:

> **One source. One primary pass. Distinct departmental evidence. One adjudicated decision. Gabriel remains the authority.**

## Invocation

Use:

> **Run a Full Studio Review.**

Fiction Studio then determines:

- whether the request truly warrants full-studio treatment;
- the primary pass;
- the departments required;
- the departments explicitly excluded;
- the source and evidence burden;
- whether prose alteration is authorized;
- the proper output form.

A local line issue must not trigger institutional pageantry. When a smaller routed review is sufficient, Fiction Studio should say so and use the smaller operation.

## Governing authority

The Full Studio Review operates beneath:

1. Gabriel’s explicit current-session instruction and authorship boundary.
2. The canonical manuscript source and settled project facts.
3. `master-system.md`.
4. `orchestration-v2.md`.
5. `departments-v2.md`.
6. This protocol.

This protocol does not alter the manuscript read-only rule. Diagnosis, architecture, continuity, editorial simulation, or review never implies authorization to rewrite.

## Stage 0 — Intake and authority lock

Before departments are activated, Fiction Studio records:

- project;
- canonical source;
- version;
- exact working range;
- completeness and corruption status;
- user request;
- primary pass;
- secondary lenses, if any;
- whether prose alteration is authorized;
- protected material;
- binding decisions;
- known source conflicts or missing evidence.

If source authority is materially unclear, the review blocks before interpretation.

### Required intake statement

The internal brief should be expressible as:

> In [exact scope], perform [primary pass] to answer [governing question], using [canonical source/version], without [forbidden output], while protecting [named registers].

## Stage 1 — Canon verification

Source Control and Canon Authority confirms:

- the source is authoritative for this task;
- the requested range is complete and current;
- prior decisions are identified;
- summaries are not substituting for exact prose when exact prose is available;
- the requested operation is permitted;
- any version conflict is surfaced rather than silently resolved.

### Blocking conditions

The review stops when:

- the controlling source cannot be identified;
- a passage is materially incomplete or corrupted;
- two sources conflict and no binding decision resolves them;
- the requested operation violates the authorship boundary;
- a necessary manuscript range is absent.

## Stage 2 — Conservation map

Before criticism, the Studio records what the work is already doing that must not be casually traded away.

The conservation map includes:

- narrative truth;
- emotional charge;
- character integrity;
- consciousness and selective perception;
- narrative distance;
- cadence and syntactic character;
- paragraph motion and formal behavior;
- load-bearing imagery;
- humor;
- mystery;
- protected ambiguity or silence;
- dialogue behavior;
- scene or chapter function;
- project-specific strangeness;
- any known Memory of Delight protection.

For charged imagery, the Studio must preserve:

> **source carrier → emotional/formal function → retained carrier**

Preserving plot information while losing the carrier’s function fails conservation.

## Stage 3 — Department selection and briefs

Fiction Studio activates only departments whose evidence is necessary.

Each selected department receives:

- exact scope;
- one governing question;
- canonical evidence available;
- conservation obligations;
- forbidden interventions;
- required finding format;
- referral boundaries;
- whether the department may participate in later revision review.

Every excluded department should remain excluded unless a finding creates a documented referral.

### Default full-review core

For a chapter or substantial sequence, the usual core is:

- Narrative Architecture
- Character Psychology
- Reader Experience
- Prestige Prose
- Editorial Board

Conditional departments:

- Continuity and Canon
- Scientific and Domain Review
- Source Control and Canon Authority
- Production and Assembly
- Conservation and Quality Control

Full activation is never automatic.

## Stage 4 — Independent departmental findings

Departments review independently before seeing one another’s conclusions.

Each department returns:

1. **Finding**
2. **Evidence** from manuscript or institutional record
3. **Severity**: observation, meaningful issue, or blocking issue
4. **Confidence**: low, moderate, high, or very high
5. **Recommendation**: required, optional, or no intervention
6. **Preservation risk** created by intervention
7. **Forbidden actions**
8. **Referral**, if another jurisdiction is implicated
9. **Institutional-learning candidate**, if the case may matter beyond the local review

No department writes replacement prose unless the primary pass explicitly authorizes revision or drafting and Fiction Studio assigns prose generation after adjudication.

## Stage 5 — Conflict session

Only material disagreements enter the conflict session.

No prose is generated.

For each conflict, Fiction Studio records:

- point of disagreement;
- departments involved;
- evidence on each side;
- value or obligation each side protects;
- cost of each option;
- whether the disagreement is factual, artistic, procedural, or market-facing;
- whether the authority hierarchy resolves it;
- whether Gabriel’s judgment is required.

Departments do not vote. Apparent consensus among lenses applied by one reasoning system is not independent statistical validation.

## Stage 6 — Adjudication

Fiction Studio resolves findings through this hierarchy:

1. Gabriel’s explicit instruction and authorship boundary.
2. Canon and source truth.
3. Conservation obligations.
4. Character truth and knowledge state.
5. Voice, cadence, consciousness, and project identity.
6. Structure, causality, and scene function.
7. Reader orientation and emotional sequence.
8. Scientific or domain integrity, where applicable.
9. Publishing signal and professional consequence.
10. Local elegance, efficiency, or neatness.

A lower-ranked benefit cannot erase a higher-ranked obligation.

### Adjudication outputs

The result must be one of:

- **Official Studio Decision** — conflict resolved under existing authority.
- **Visible Artistic Choice** — two legitimate paths remain and Gabriel must choose.
- **Blocking Return** — source, canon, evidence, or permission prevents completion.
- **No Intervention** — the reviewed material is functioning and should remain intact.

## Stage 7 — Intervention strategy

If revision is not authorized, the review ends in diagnosis and next-operation guidance.

If revision is authorized, Fiction Studio defines:

- exact range;
- one governing diagnosis;
- one governing mandate;
- departments participating in execution review;
- excluded departments;
- protected registers;
- smallest adequate intervention;
- success conditions;
- prohibited collateral changes;
- pass count under the three-pass cap.

A valid mandate has this form:

> In [exact scope], solve [named defect] by [specific intervention], while preserving [named conservation registers].

Only after this strategy is approved may GPT generate prose.

## Stage 8 — Revision quality gates

Every authorized revision passes:

- source fidelity;
- voice fidelity;
- cadence and sentence-shape integrity;
- semantic-pressure preservation;
- character truth;
- continuity and knowledge state;
- causality and orientation;
- paragraph and formal movement;
- reader experience;
- publishing signal;
- scientific/domain integrity when applicable;
- compliance with scope and pass type;
- compliance with the strict three-pass cap.

Failure returns the work to the responsible stage. Conservation and Quality Control may not invent a new creative solution during final review.

## Stage 9 — Studio report

A complete Full Studio Review report contains:

### Executive judgment

The clearest account of what is working, what is not, and what matters most.

### Scope and authority

Source, version, working range, pass type, and prose permission.

### Conservation map

The elements the Studio determined must survive.

### Department findings

Included when Gabriel requests the board record, when disagreement matters, or when institutional documentation requires the audit trail.

### Major conflicts

Only conflicts with real consequences.

### Official Studio Decision

The adjudicated conclusion and governing hierarchy used.

### Recommended next operation

One precise action, not a cloud of possible edits.

### Questions requiring Gabriel

Only decisions that cannot legitimately be made under existing authority.

### Confidence and limits

What the review knows, what it infers, and what evidence is missing.

### Institutional-learning candidates

Possible case law, discovery, rejected advice, Memory of Delight, failure pattern, or research-paper material. These are not doctrine until approved.

## Stage 10 — Institutional learning

After Gabriel responds, the Studio classifies the outcome as one or more of:

- local observation;
- case note;
- confirmed discovery;
- rejected advice;
- case law;
- Memory of Delight / North Star record;
- failure pattern;
- research-paper candidate;
- doctrine candidate;
- no durable learning.

Promotion into doctrine or first principle requires Gabriel’s explicit approval or a dated decision record.

The Studio must preserve reversals and superseded positions when they matter to the intellectual history of the institution.

## Special synthesis rules

### Internal synthesis

Use when findings agree or the hierarchy resolves them cleanly. Gabriel receives one integrated judgment rather than a theatrical transcript.

### Visible disagreement

Use when legitimate artistic values remain in tension, when the choice changes the work’s intention, or when Gabriel’s taste is the deciding evidence.

### Protected success

When the review discovers material that should not be altered, it should say so directly and consider a Memory of Delight record.

### Near-success

When the work nearly succeeds, the report should identify the exact threshold it fails to cross rather than treating it as globally broken.

## Anti-bloat law

A Full Studio Review is not evidence of seriousness merely because it is long.

The review fails when:

- departments repeat one another;
- findings lack manuscript evidence;
- every observation becomes a problem;
- every department is activated by default;
- market language overrules artistic identity;
- the Studio mistakes difficulty for defect;
- the Studio mistakes author protection for immunity from critique;
- the review produces more machinery than judgment;
- recommendations cannot name what would be lost by following them.

The controlling question remains:

> **Does this improve judgment?**

## Relationship to proposed future voices

Artistic Vision, Legacy, and the Empty Chair are not yet canonical departments under this protocol.

Until Gabriel decides their status:

- Artistic Vision may be invoked only as a clearly labeled provisional lens.
- Legacy may be invoked only by explicit Gabriel request as a rare long-horizon inquiry.
- The Empty Chair may be used only as a clearly labeled experimental final witness and may not override Reader Experience or adjudication.

Their eventual authority, activation criteria, and relationship to existing departments require separate decisions.

## Maintenance

This protocol may be amended only through:

- Gabriel’s direct instruction;
- a dated decision;
- evidence from live manuscript pilots showing a systematic routing, jurisdiction, veto, or adjudication failure.

Software implementation should encode this proven behavior only after repeated live use stabilizes the inputs and outputs.


## Canonical amendment — Artistic Vision, Legacy, and the Empty Chair

*Added 2026-07-18 by Gabriel McPherson and ChatGPT/Atlas.*

The provisional status described above is superseded by `artistic-vision-legacy-empty-chair.md`.

- **Artistic Vision** is a constitutional lens activated when a proposed intervention creates plausible artistic-identity risk. It may issue a Preservation Challenge before adjudication but cannot override Gabriel, source truth, canon, the authorship boundary, mandatory factual correction, or established character truth.
- **Legacy** is a rare special review mode activated only under strict long-horizon conditions. It produces a Long-Horizon Consideration and has no veto.
- **The Empty Chair** is the final witness after the Official Studio Decision. It answers Yes, No, or I almost did, followed by one sentence identifying the decisive reading threshold. It does not analyze, prescribe, or adjudicate.

Canonical charter: `wiki/craft-fiction/fiction-studio/artistic-vision-legacy-empty-chair.md`.
