6. **The mesh must be visible before the reveal but not fully named too early.** Early symptoms can include timing glitches, marigold scent, system choices, docking irregularity, and too-clean evidence.
7. **Soren must graduate from intimate voice to public architect.** The reader should feel the private manipulation widen into institutional takeover.
8. **Jalen's final transmission must re-key the plot.** The point is not only that Jalen dies; it is that he names the real architecture.
9. **The re-entry into consolidated late material requires a new premise.** After Jalen's sacrifice, the story is no longer "catch the smugglers"; it is "retake a living station from the inside."
