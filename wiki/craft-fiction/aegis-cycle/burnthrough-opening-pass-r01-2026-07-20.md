## Sample Execution: Prologue Scene 2 Opening

This sample applies the duplicate-heading fix and the proposed administrator replacement. It does not alter the manuscript source.

```text
2. Bounty

Chamber 17 of the Vaylos Span Civil Services Building wasn't the kind of room that tolerated outbursts. The walls were too smooth, the light too soft. It was a place designed to make compromise look civilized.

But the voices inside had frayed.

"Twenty-seven percent," the Archival Consortium's senior liaison snapped, tapping her tablet with a perfectly manicured finger. "That's how much you've diverted from stationwide data processing to this... ghost-chasing. Do you understand what that does to transfer capacity on protected archives?"

Someone from Central Security started to answer, but was cut off by a polite ping.

At the head of the table, Magistrate Edran Vossk glanced down at his wrist. The display lit briefly, enough for one line of red text.

INTERCEPTION FAILED. TARGET: IN TRANSIT.
```

Assessment: This passes the local gate. It removes the accidental duplicate heading, eliminates the Star Wars-adjacent name, and preserves the institutional coldness, interruption, and threat arrival.
