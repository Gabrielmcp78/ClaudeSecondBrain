## Current Canon Support Summary

The backstory layer makes the Chain a civilization founded on a public prohibition and a private dependency. Sehrava 3's distributed, redundant empirical technology and Sehrava 4's elegant, centralized biophotonic technology became political philosophies before becoming existential survival systems. The Twin Stars crisis, the failed attempt to stabilize the stars, and the evacuation reduced the civilization to the Chain.

The hidden isotope runs are central. The Chain publicly treats biophotonic technology and Harmonic Isotope use as forbidden lessons from catastrophe, while privately depending on carefully managed isotope retrieval and cryonic transfer to keep the civilization alive. This makes the cryonic cargo, Erevos command secrecy, and Draven's institutional burden structurally connected rather than merely plot devices.

The worldbuilding files define a larger antagonist ecology around the Celerian Collective / Consortium / Swarm material. These labels may not yet be harmonized. Before manuscript-wide diagnosis, reconcile whether "Celerian Collective," "Consortium," and "Swarm" are separate entities, nested entities, historical phases, or draft-name drift.

The outposts note adds a tonal counterweight: the Chain is not only decay, smuggling, and militarized scarcity. Several stations preserve credible domestic, artistic, agricultural, educational, spiritual, and working-class futures. This matters for Kieran/Draven because "settling down" has plausible places, not only an abstract postwar wish.

## Determinations — 2026-07-20

### Consolidated 2.3 is a full-arc container, not a fully executed current draft

`Burnthrough_Draft_2.3.consolodate.docx` carries the broad book arc through late headings including Chapter Seventeen: The Revelation, Chapter Eighteen: Final Battle, and Chapter Nineteen: Choices. It should therefore be treated as the current full-arc container.

It is not, however, complete as executed prose. The clearest proof is the literal placeholder in Chapter Ten: `Jalen sacrifice comes here.` This means the consolidated draft preserves the intended location of the Jalen sacrifice but does not execute that beat. It also has no exact paragraph overlap with the 3.0 `Soren's Revelation.docx` material.

### Jalen scandal and death arc is not fully present in consolidated 2.3

The consolidated file includes Jalen's earlier reintroduction and later references, but it does not contain the full scandal / arrest / death sequence as a complete prose arc.

The 3.0 alternate split contains the missing Jalen execution layer:

- `Ch 3.docx` introduces Jalen Myrr as a scene-level presence.
- `Ch 5.docx` reactivates Jalen as a live pressure point.
- `Ch 10.docx` executes the scandal leak, public frame, Jalen's arrest, Kieran/Draven confrontation, Kieran's fall, and waking aftermath.
- `Ch 11.docx` carries the post-arrest investigation and confirms the case against Jalen is engineered, with Dock 9 as the first structural crack.
- `Ch 12.docx` carries the listener aftermath and biophotonic / swarm analysis.
- `Ch 13.docx` carries the visible lure / strategic trap pressure while Jalen remains politically buried.
- `Ch 15.docx` executes Jalen's prison-transport sequence, his recognition that the mesh is using continuity, his evacuation of others, final transmission to Kieran, and sacrificial cascade that opens the gap back into Erevos.

Therefore: the Jalen scandal and death arc exists in 3.0 alternate material, but it has not been integrated into consolidated 2.3. Ch 14 in the 3.0 alternate folder is a stub, so there is still a bridge / numbering gap between the Ch 13 lure state and the Ch 15 death sequence.

### Soren twist arc exists as an insert chain, not as one finished chapter

The Soren twist is distributed across the 3.0 alternate package and cannot be imported from `Soren's Revelation.docx` alone.

Required insert chain:

1. `Ch 4.docx` — Soren's first direct contact in the lift after the Night Market material.

3. `Ch 7.docx` and `Ch. 9.docx` — Dock 9, the Solace wreck, convoy / ghost-ship escalation, and the lead-in to the fault-line aftermath.

6. `Ch 12.docx` — listener extraction, proof of biophotonic / swarm coordination, and operational implications.

8. `Ch 15.docx` — Jalen's death revealing that Dock Nine, the Solace accident, and the route-continuity system are the root, not the visible scandal.
9. `Soren's Revelation.docx` — architecture for Soren's broadcast, Dock Nine root corridor, cracked cryo-capsule, root purge, civilian uprising, Command advance, and six-month-earlier seed image.

`Soren's Revelation.docx` is not a clean manuscript source. It begins with an AI-process note and ends with a "What Changed in This Pass" note. Treat it as architecture / prose-draft evidence only until it is stripped, expanded, reconciled with chapter numbering, and rewritten or approved as a clean manuscript unit.
