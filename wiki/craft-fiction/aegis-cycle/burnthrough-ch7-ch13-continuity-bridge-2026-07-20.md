## Governing Determination

Chapters 7-13 form the correct continuity bridge from physical Dock 9 evidence into political narrative warfare and strategic displacement. The sequence should be protected as architecture before any line pass or assembly.

Its job is not only to escalate danger. Its job is to prove that the antagonist pattern has moved through four layers at once:

1. **Material layer:** Dock 9, the Solace wreck, isotope gel, metallic / organic bloom, dead relays, and biophotonic lattice.
2. **Ship layer:** the ghost ship attacks records, memory, sensor confidence, and convoy safety.
3. **Public layer:** the humanitarian scandal turns relief logistics into a weaponized story against Jalen and Kieran.
4. **Intimate layer:** Soren uses history, desire, shame, and Draven's love as tactical infrastructure.

The bridge works because each chapter changes the kind of evidence available. Ch. 7 makes the threat physical and impossible. Ch. 8 lets the crew become emotionally legible after mass loss. Ch. 9 proves the enemy is herding, not raiding. Ch. 10 weaponizes public narrative. Ch. 11 violates Kieran privately and plants the listener. Ch. 12 converts violation into proof of distributed assembly. Ch. 13 uses visible warships to draw the Aegis away from Erevos while Jalen remains politically buried.

The chapters are not ready for assembly as-is. They need locked terminology cleanup, command-rank consolidation, careful duplication/hierarchy review, and a later seam decision against consolidated 2.3 and the Ch. 15 Jalen sacrifice.
