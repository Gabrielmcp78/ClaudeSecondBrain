## Overview

Gabriel's compositional work spans theatre, film, television, and recorded music, operating in parallel with his directing career rather than subordinate to it. The body of work — five commissioned musicals, 20+ theatrical scores, film/TV composition, and eight years co-leading a nationally touring rock band — reflects a composer who moves across formal and popular idioms without treating either as primary.

The crossover between composition and his theoretical work (VCH, GHRM) is not incidental. The 68.48 Hz entrainment mechanic in *String Theory* is only legible as a fictional mechanism because Gabriel built the musical science behind it. The Shakespearience uses original commissioned music as a structural element of the listening experience, not accompaniment.
