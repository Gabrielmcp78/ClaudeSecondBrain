## Directing Philosophy

The documented approach to Shakespeare is grounded in First Folio speech truth — the text as actor's score, not as literary artifact. From the existing pedagogy article:

**Central method:** The "3-E" system — Examine the text at the syllabic level, Embody the physical impulse before adding intellectual interpretation, Execute with commitment to the discovered truth. This sequence makes Shakespeare accessible without making it simpler.

**Objective formula:** "My character wants [specific verb] from [specific other character] because [specific reason rooted in text evidence]."

**Anti-decoration principle:** Truthful acting is the throughline; theatrical ornamentation is a consequence, not a goal.

See [Acting and Performance Pedagogy](acting-and-performance-pedagogy.md) for the full documented framework.

## The Shakespearience — Direction by Proxy

Gabriel is not the narrator or director of individual Shakespearience episodes (those are performance/casting roles for Stephanie Carll's production). His directing contribution is architectural: the production philosophy, the pedagogical progression across age tiers, the principle that performance-driven storytelling and language-centered production serve children's Shakespeare better than adaptation-for-children in the reductive sense.

That architectural contribution draws directly from his 60+ productions and 350+ master classes — the Shakespearience is the distillation of that body of work into a platform rather than another production.
