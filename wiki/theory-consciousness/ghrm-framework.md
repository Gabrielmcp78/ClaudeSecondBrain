# Geometric-Harmonic Resonance Model (GHRM)

*Gabriel McPherson's theoretical physics framework proposing that fundamental unification is accessible via harmonic and geometric coherence rather than kinetic energy thresholds. All claims in this article are labeled `[theoretical]` per the knowledge base style guide, as this constitutes Gabriel's originated theoretical position. The model is documented in a formal monograph co-authored with validation code, with theoretical foundations drawn from Robert Edward Grant's Unity Harmonica (UH) framework.* (source: GHRM-LHC Monograph: Validation of the Fundamental λ5D Coupling Constant via High-Coherence Resonance.md)

## Core Thesis [theoretical]

The GHRM challenges the **Brute Energy Hypothesis** — the dominant assumption in contemporary high-energy physics that increasingly profound physical principles reveal themselves only through surmounting kinetic energy thresholds (e.g., reaching the Planck scale). The GHRM proposes the **Harmonic Resonance Thesis** as an alternative: fundamental unification is accessible not through energy maximization but through **geometric and harmonic coherence** with the underlying mathematical architecture of the cosmos.

The practical implication is that the GHRM experimental paradigm does not maximize collision energy (E) but instead maximizes **coherence and precise phase alignment** between relativistic time dilation (γ) and an externally imposed harmonic field (f). The particle becomes a resonator tuned to geometric constants derived from the Unity Harmonica framework. [theoretical]
