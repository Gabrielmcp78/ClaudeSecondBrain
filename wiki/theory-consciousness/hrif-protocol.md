HRIF organizes all output and engagement across three tiers:

**Tertiary Synthesis (9):** Nine integration insights close the loop — interrelations across fields, timeframes, and systems. Invites inquiry, stillness, or action.

This 3-6-9 mapping is the same architecture used in Gabriel's sentence-grouping and paragraph proportion standards. It is not stylistic preference — it is structural law within the HRIF system.
