## Authorized seams and executed changes

   - Added that David checked the time twice, grounding the later reconstruction in an actual remembered observation.

2. **Balcony transition**
   - Replaced the abstract sentence `By the time resistance occurred to him, the balcony door was open.`

3. **Duration after the family’s laughter**
   - Removed the explicit interpretation of David as unguarded, foolish, yielding, and caught by pleasure.
   - Continued the family’s movement and allowed David to remain at the railing, smiling, before the book falls.
   - The scene now carries non-instrumental attention through duration rather than thesis.

4. **Hand and cold legibility**
   - Clarified that David can see the left hand and arm but cannot feel or command them.
   - Separated the right hand’s cold adhesion to the iron from the left arm’s neurological failure.
   - Preserved the anomalous cold and David’s mistaken belief that he has been outside less than a minute.

5. **Watch introduction and missing-time causality**
   - Introduced the watch as an object David still thinks of as Arthur’s before expanding its lineage.
   - Reframed David’s uncertainty about when he began wearing it as ordinary funeral-period dislocation rather than a second unexplained anomaly.
   - Changed `The time was wrong` to `The time could not be right`, preserving David’s perception while avoiding the false implication that the watch malfunctioned.
   - Grounded the arithmetic in the time checked at the apartment door.
