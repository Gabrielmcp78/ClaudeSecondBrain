## Exact scope

Opening sentence through `David went inside.` Everything after that sentence was preserved verbatim from 7.2.1.

## Governing mandate

Rebuild the opening as a relational field rather than a chain of isolated significance points. Allow the narrator to move among David, family, architecture, weather, sound, object, inheritance, and institutional geography while maintaining reader footing. Make David's non-instrumental pleasure in watching the family the emotional substance of the missing fifteen minutes. Clarify the left-arm event as a visible but unresponsive limb, not a physically vanished hand. Introduce Arthur's watch through recognition, material history, and ordinary grief before it becomes the instrument revealing anomalous time.

## Council lenses

- Gabriel McPherson: narrator as field; resonance through minor coherences; beauty without compulsory utility.
- Virginia Woolf: elastic but legible narrative distance.
- Richard Powers: interacting systems and emergent event.
- Toni Morrison: object, inheritance, language, and place carrying history.
- Kazuo Ishiguro: preserve David's defenses and prevent premature confession.
- Anthony Minghella: stage meaning through sound, gesture, space, and transition before interpretation.
- Don DeLillo, targeted: institutional geography, machinery, cold precision, and wry pressure.

## Principal interventions

1. Established that David checked the watch at the door before remembering the book, making the later calculation causally legible.
2. Expanded East Hill, the apartment, the balcony, the winter acoustics, the town, and Cornell into the scene's active field.
3. Removed the abstract transition `By the time resistance occurred to him` and allowed David to cross outside without inventing a scientific pretext.
4. Preserved the family's strongest existing gestures while giving the observation duration after they leave.
5. Made explicit, without forcing confession, that the scene offered no problem or use and that David nevertheless remained.
6. Rebuilt the left-arm anomaly so David sees the hand, commands it, and receives no movement or sensation. No disappearance is implied.
7. Reduced competing two-hand choreography and retained the iron/cold as environmental evidence.
8. Introduced the watch through the cuff catching on the book, Arthur's prior ownership, Elliot's lineage, and the morgue memory.
9. Distinguished ordinary grief-related memory loss from the speculative anomaly by grounding the post-funeral interval in receipts and mileage.
10. Made the fifteen-minute discovery proceed from prior watch check, hand event, watch recognition, mechanical observation, arithmetic, family-distance confirmation, and Cornell's continuing schedule.
11. Gave David the unnerving recognition that he had watched and enjoyed the family without producing a question, theory, or useful result.
