## Eight Movements

**I. Chorale — Wednesday Dinner:** David arrives late to ritual Wednesday supper with Celeste (the tradition has continued 387 days after their romantic dissolution). He admits "92.7 percent certainty" he'd attend regardless of exhaustion. Celeste notices his tremor; suggests he play music for pleasure rather than equation-hunting. David agrees to explore her Pythagorean tuning theory.

**II. Arioso — The Tuning:** They play until midnight, improvising with Celeste's modified harmonic intervals. David sleeps seven unbroken hours — first genuine rest in weeks.

**III. Recitativum — The Office:** Morning clarity dissolves. By noon David believes he has achieved breakthrough unification: mapping π to electromagnetic spectrum, φ to harmonic frequencies, e to temporal coefficients. Meets Rune Ishikawa at 2:30 p.m.

**IV. Turba — The Guillotine:** Ishikawa dismantles David's work systematically. Diagnosis: mathematical pareidolia — "cleverly arranged unrelated objects creating appearance of connection." He produces a dossier documenting David's decline: poor evaluations, missed conferences, failed publications. Reveals his own youthful error (Fibonacci/dark matter) as false-comfort, then delivers: administrative leave is being recommended.

**V. Via Dolorosa — Into the Storm:** David flees into November sleet carrying demolished work and cello case.

**VI. Crucifixio — It Takes a Beating:** In a darkened underpass, two assailants rob him of wallet, phone, and notebook. They kick him repeatedly, leaving him bloodied on wet pavement.

**VII. Depositio/Sepultura — Up From the Depths:** An elderly woman in scarves appears, tends his wounds, speaks cryptically about patterns and broken symmetry. Produces a small, precisely-machined tuning fork; places it in his hand: "You tune the world... But your head cannot hold it." She vanishes at dawn.

**VIII. Reliquia — Up From the Depths:** David strikes the fork against metal. Instead of concert A, it produces a recursive, impossible harmonic — a shimmer that generates a visible magenta color in the rainwater. His cello case resonates in sympathy. The fork leaves a blue mark on his palm. He walks home altered, "vectorized," perceiving the city's invisible grid with new clarity.

## Key Elements

- **Ishikawa's dossier:** First concrete institutional action against David; administrative leave recommended
- **The elderly woman:** Identity unknown; source of the tuning fork; her appearance after the assault is possibly non-naturalistic [unverified]
- **The tuning fork:** Precisely machined, not commercially standard; produces magenta light and a blue palm mark; the cello resonates in sympathy
- **Mathematical pareidolia:** Ishikawa's characterization of David's breakthrough — a term that may be accurate or may be suppression
- **The passion form:** The movement titles (Chorale, Arioso, Recitativum, Turba, Via Dolorosa, Crucifixio, Depositio/Sepultura, Reliquia) map directly onto the eight movements of the Bach/Handel oratorio passion tradition, framing David's suffering as a secular martyrdom
