## Structure

Two interleaved timelines:
- Present day: Buffalo (conference) and Cornell
- 2005 flashback: MIT / Boston

The fugue structure mirrors musical form — themes introduced in one timeline recur transformed in the other. Past and present are "phase-locked around the same underlying phenomenon."

---

## Present Day (Buffalo / Cornell)

David arrives at a scientific conference in Buffalo. Encounters Dr. Julian Kline (NYU astrophysicist) — who responds to David's recognition with visible dread.

David discovers an instrument repair shop run by **E. Guare** (= Eleanor Guare), who displays cryptic familiarity with him. Opening his cello case reveals internal repair marks from 1985 bearing his grandfather Elliot Lang's initials.

A late-night call with his mother Cassandra Lang reveals that his grandfather possessed the same mysterious melody David has unconsciously known his entire life.

Back at Cornell, David teaches a physics class where reality becomes increasingly unstable: fluorescent lights pulse in rhythm with his heartbeat, colors flash at the periphery of vision, his classroom equations drift into musical notation without his conscious control.

Walking empty corridors afterward: temporal disorientation and acoustic phenomena distributed throughout the building's infrastructure (pipes, vents, walls). The distinction between external sound and internal perception collapses.

Final image: David uncertain whether his watch reads 92 seconds, 41 hours, or "one minute, forty-six seconds before time."

## 2005 Flashback (MIT / Boston)

Young David, Marcus McGrath, and Celeste Armitage argue about consciousness as the underlying language of both music and mathematics. Their theoretical work on acoustic resonance attracts attention from Dr. Rune Ishikawa, who arrives at a chaotic bonfire scene where Marcus is destroying their research papers.

Ishikawa reveals David's presentation to Dr. Kline triggered serious institutional concern. He demands all three bring materials to his Cornell office — "their work touches something dangerous that requires careful handling."

## Characters

**David Lang** — Physicist grappling with discovery connecting consciousness, resonance, and harmonic mathematics. Haunted by inherited knowledge from grandfather.

**Elliot Lang** — David's grandfather (deceased). Possessed the same mysterious melody. Specialized knowledge about acoustic phenomena. His initials appear in cello repair marks from 1985.

**Cassandra Lang** — David's mother, still living. Remembers her father-in-law's secretive behavior about the melody.

**E. Guare / Eleanor Guare** — Proprietor of instrument repair shop in Buffalo. Displays unexplained connection to David's family.

**Dr. Julian Kline** — NYU astrophysicist. His reaction to David's research triggered institutional concern in 2005.

**Rune Ishikawa** — Arrives at the 2005 bonfire to contain the research. Demands all materials be brought to Cornell.

**Marcus McGrath** — Third collaborator. In 2005, attempts to suppress the work through destruction (burning papers).

**Celeste Armitage** — Third collaborator. Attempting preservation and mediation in 2005.

## Key Mechanics

**The inherited melody** — possessed by Elliot Lang, unconsciously known by David his entire life. A transmission across generations.

**The 3:2 ratio / intervals that "hold but do not open"** — mathematical structures that resist resolution; recur as both acoustic and theoretical references.

**Temporal destabilization** — David's watch reads impossibly ambiguous times; subjective time and clock time diverge.

**Unresolved harmonic frequency** — functions as literal phenomenon and symbol for incomplete knowledge seeking completion.
