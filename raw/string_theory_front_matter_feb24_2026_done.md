## BEFORE THE FIRST WORD

Before form, there was only ratio.
Not as law. Not as god.
As the only thing that could not be otherwise.

And the ratio moved upon the silence.
And the ratio divided the silence from itself.
And the division became an interval.
And the interval was the first boundary.

And from the boundary, form was permitted —
not created, but recognized.
Each thing assigned the ratio it could keep.
And in this way, the many emerged from the one.

And the ratio did not depart from its forms.
It remained within them, waiting.
Waiting for the moment when one form might remember it.
And this remembrance was called resonance.

And when one form sounded its ratio purely, the others answered.
Not by command.
By recognition.
For they had always known each other.

And this was the first harmony.

And the harmony did not belong to the form —
the form belonged to the harmony.
And through harmony, the form became itself fully.
