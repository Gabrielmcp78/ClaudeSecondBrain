## Chapter 13: Phenom de Guare
**Words:** 5,695 | **Scenes:** 7
**Timeline:** November 29, 2026 (with 1971 flashback)

An EMP at 2:47 a.m. unexpectedly leaves major research facilities operational and enhanced. A 47-minute holographic recording arrives simultaneously at facilities worldwide, containing compression algorithms that rewrite equipment firmware in real-time.

Origin point: Ithaca, New York.

Marcus McGrath (UCLA) coordinates global scientific response through emergency channels, connecting CERN, Fermilab, MIT, JPL. Dr. Mira Kovács at CERN detects Monster group symmetries in detector data.

Ishikawa reaches David's lecture hall and finds Celeste transfixed before chalkboards covered in glowing equations — David's mathematical frameworks from before his suspension.

Celeste articulates the revelation: "The universe isn't broken. It's singing." The equations represent consciousness treated as frequency. David has "octavated" — shifted to a higher dimensional register of reality, his consciousness-integrated mathematics operating in eleven-dimensional space.

The system begins anticipating dialogue: "The future's bleeding backward."

**1971 flashback:** Young Rune Ishikawa accesses a secret lab buried beneath Cornell's East Hill through hidden passages. He finds Dr. Kenji Tsukino has vanished mid-equation. Ishikawa seals the lab from within, maintaining its mysterious operation. A telegram hints at cryptic coordination with Julian Kline.

McGrath authorizes encryption and off-registry archival under code name **Project Cantus** (from *cantus perpetuus* — the everlasting song). NSA seals close over the channels. A planetary harmonic resonance hums through power lines.

## Chapter 14: Ratios of One
**Words:** 1,510 | **Setting:** November 30, 2026, 3:48 a.m.

David enters a dream-like scholar's study — books and manuscripts ordered according to a private system. He discovers a journal containing philosophical ratios and the word "resonance."

His father Arthur appears, playing violin — Bach's Chaconne. Arthur has preserved David's rejected papers and abandoned theories in labeled binders: **"David's Big Swings."** His philosophy: "That's where a mind lives. Not in the finished thing. In the reach."

Final exchange: "You were always welcome." The reconciliation David could never achieve in life occurs here.

## Chapter 15: The Pivotal Encounter
**Words:** 3,165

David wakes on the East Hill bench at dawn. A six-minute walk down campus takes hours; the sky suggests minutes have passed.

At Hamid's corner store: a clementine and newspaper instead of his usual ramen and donuts. Temporal distortion intensifies: sound withdraws mid-street, the clementine suspends in air, a figure appears by his building before vanishing. Doors unlock independently.

**Embedded New York Times articles:**
- President Elaine Marsh convenes emergency joint session re: solar-electromagnetic event that knocked out digital infrastructure on six continents. Marcus McGrath appointed Special Presidential Envoy.
- **"The Cellist"**: A 47-minute viral video shows an unseen figure playing cello. Four billion viewers cannot adequately describe the experience. Spectrographic analysis reveals impossible harmonic structures encoding fundamental constants of physics. The performer becomes "increasingly indistinct" before disappearing entirely in the final frames.

David appears unaware of having created the viral video.

## Chapter 16: The Final Decision
**Subtitle:** passacaglia with chorale in four movements | **Words:** 6,604

**Movement I:** David discovers a note from an unnamed woman with cryptic guidance about choosing between visibility and cosmic significance. Attorney Charles P. Vermasian describes impossible financial returns from the viral broadcast — algorithmically distorted, suggesting forces beyond conventional economics.

**Movement II:** David composes an elaborate passacaglia embedding physical constants (Avogadro's, fine-structure, Planck's length) into musical notation. 200+ measures of staff paper, creating a hybrid mathematics-music document. Designed to encode himself into the structure while remaining personally anonymous.

**Movement III:** David meets Celeste at the Music School's upper loft. She carries his child — a son. David's body literally flickers between states, unable to maintain human form. Celeste: "Leave while you still have agency." He touches the place where the child lives. She gives him a photograph of a frost-covered tuning fork marked "Someday" on the reverse.

**Movement IV:** David positions his completed score beside his cello case. The apartment walls remain covered in equations — left deliberately for future seekers.
